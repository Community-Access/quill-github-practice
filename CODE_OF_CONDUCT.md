# Code of conduct

People come here to learn, often for the first time and often with a screen
reader. Please make that easy for them.

- Be kind and patient, especially with questions you think are simple.
- Write plainly. Describe what you mean in words rather than with images, and
  give every link a name that says where it goes.
- Don't post anything you wouldn't want a new learner to read.

Community Access looks after this repository and may edit or remove anything
that doesn't fit these rules. To raise a concern privately, email
support@community-access.org.
